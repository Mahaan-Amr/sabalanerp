## What to build

Create the reusable neutral neumorphic workflow frame and prove it through the Sales landing and the first shared Sales Contract wizard step. The Sales landing keeps its existing actions and routes while redundant visible descriptions and the quick-access heading are removed through reusable component capabilities. Normal creation, collaboration creation, and contract editing share the same wizard frame, centered progress icons and connectors, stable RTL navigation controls, and Step 1 presentation without changing any workflow behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Domain-neutral workflow frame, progress, navigation, and surface capabilities are reusable from the platform design system.
- [ ] Contract labels, step definitions, handlers, and rules remain inside the contract-creation feature.
- [ ] The Sales landing retains all five actions and routes while removing card descriptions and the redundant visible quick-access heading.
- [ ] Normal creation, collaboration creation, and editing render the same shared wizard frame.
- [ ] Step 1 retains its existing values, probable-number behavior, validation, and safety guidance.
- [ ] Wizard icons and completed checks are centered in consistent containers and connectors share their center axis.
- [ ] Next remains right, Previous remains left, and the counter remains centered.
- [ ] Next and Previous labels and arrows stay on one centered RTL row in the approved visual order.
- [ ] Loading indicators do not shift button dimensions or labels.
- [ ] Light and dark desktop/mobile behavior is covered at the shared acceptance seam.
- [ ] No business rule, route, API, persisted meaning, permission, recovery, or submission behavior changes.
