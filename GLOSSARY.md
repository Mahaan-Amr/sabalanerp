# Customer Accounting

Terms for settling a customer's open obligations with posted monetary sources.

## Language

**Customer Source Share**:
A specified portion of one posted receipt or independent customer credit assigned to one open customer obligation. Unassigned value remains with its original source.
_Avoid_: payment transfer, automatic offset

**Grouped Customer Allocation**:
One confirmed settlement that assigns shares from one or more customer sources to one or more open obligations as a single financial event.
_Avoid_: batch of separate payments

**Independent Customer Credit**:
Posted customer value available for settlement that does not represent a treasury receipt.
_Avoid_: synthetic receipt

**Pending Allocation Intent**:
The exact proposed grouped allocation whose posting outcome has not yet been established for the user.
_Avoid_: failed allocation, posted allocation

**Allocation Reversal**:
An immutable correction linked to a posted allocation that returns its shares to their original sources and reopens the corresponding obligations.
_Avoid_: deletion, edit of the original allocation
