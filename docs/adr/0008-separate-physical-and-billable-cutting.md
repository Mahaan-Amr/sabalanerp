# Separate physical and billable cutting

Cutting keeps both its physical production truth and its billable sales charge because the two legitimately diverge for حکمی products. Workshop and remaining-stone calculations retain the physical operation and calculated internal cost, while customer, accounting, summary, and invoice totals use only the billable cutting charge; finalized historical contracts change only through an explicit edit and save.
