# Bill longitudinal but not cross cutting for mandatory products

حکمی changes material pricing but does not waive longitudinal cutting labor: every actual longitudinal cut remains customer-billable, while cross cutting remains non-billable. Both cut types retain their physical meters and internal costs for workshop and remaining-stone truth, and finalized historical contracts adopt this rule only through an authorized explicit edit and save. This supersedes ADR-0008's blanket zero billable cutting rule for حکمی products.
