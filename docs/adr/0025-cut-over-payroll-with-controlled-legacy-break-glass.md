# Cut over payroll with controlled legacy break-glass access

At payroll cutover the new HR system becomes the only ordinary writer, while legacy editing is retained temporarily only as audited, least-privilege break-glass access. Permanent removal follows two parallel periods, one fully reconciled live payroll, a 30-day critical-discrepancy-free window, and joint HR Payroll, Accounting, and System Owner approval, balancing recovery capability against the risk of dual sources of truth.
