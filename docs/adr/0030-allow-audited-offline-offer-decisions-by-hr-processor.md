# Allow audited offline offer decisions by the HR Processor

When a Candidate cannot complete the portal decision, the assigned HR Processor may record the Candidate’s acceptance or decline alone after the latest offer is fully approved. This deliberately favors operational continuity over a second internal approver, while keeping the action distinct from a Candidate portal decision and requiring immutable evidence: communication method and time, the reason for using the offline path, confirmed Candidate information, an explanatory note, and the recording HR Processor’s identity.

## Consequences

Offline records are never represented as Candidate-authenticated actions and cannot be silently edited in place. A correction requires a new audited decision event; direct portal decisions remain the preferred path.
