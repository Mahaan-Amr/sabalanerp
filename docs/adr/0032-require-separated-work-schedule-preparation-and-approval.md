# Require separated work-schedule preparation and approval

The responsible supervisor may propose a direct report's work-schedule change, an HR Processor prepares the effective-dated draft, and a separate HR Manager approves or returns it; only approval creates the canonical schedule version. This retains Human Resources ownership from ADR-0017 while replacing immediate generic HR edits with accountable separation of duties, without granting mutation authority to Security, Payroll, employees, or unrelated managers.
