# Normalize Legacy No-Discount Evidence at Financial Approval

Legacy Sales wizard contracts used an explicit null discount snapshot to mean that no discount was applied, while older contracts may omit the field entirely. Financial approval normalizes explicit null to immutable zero-discount evidence and normalizes an absent field only when the frozen payable total exactly reconciles with the frozen canonical gross total and no conflicting commercial adjustment exists. Within that legacy no-discount boundary, omitted product `isLayer` flags retain the wizard's historical non-layer meaning and are recorded as normalized eligibility evidence; explicit layers remain excluded, while malformed or conflicting evidence stays fail-closed. This avoids commercially meaningful contract resaves and invoice-snapshot invalidation while preserving normalization origins and reconciliation facts in the approved-pricing evidence.
