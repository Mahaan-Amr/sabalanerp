# Normalize Legacy No-Discount Evidence at Financial Approval

Legacy Sales wizard contracts used an explicit null discount snapshot to mean that no discount was applied, while older contracts may omit the field entirely. Financial approval normalizes explicit null to immutable zero-discount evidence and normalizes an absent field only when the frozen payable total exactly reconciles with the frozen canonical gross total and no conflicting commercial adjustment exists. Within that legacy no-discount boundary, omitted product `isLayer` flags retain the wizard's historical non-layer meaning and are recorded as normalized eligibility evidence; explicit layers remain excluded, while malformed or conflicting evidence stays fail-closed. This avoids commercially meaningful contract resaves and invoice-snapshot invalidation while preserving normalization origins and reconciliation facts in the approved-pricing evidence.

## Explicit zero-discount snapshots with historical omissions

An explicit disabled discount with zero amount and percent may also carry historical omitted `isLayer` flags. Financial approval may normalize those omissions only when frozen payable and gross totals exactly reconcile and no conflicting discount or non-product adjustment evidence exists. Malformed flags and contradictory layer metadata remain blocked; explicit layers stay excluded. When an omission is recovered, a stale zero-discount base subtotal is replaced by the canonical eligible base solely in the immutable approved-pricing evidence. That evidence retains the original basis, reconciliation totals, normalization origin, and recovered row identities. The source Contract, applied discount, payable amount, and selected-item scope are unchanged. Positive discounts and ordinary explicit eligibility continue to require exact saved-basis agreement.

## Active discounts and audited product classification

For a positive saved discount, historical omitted product classification may be recovered from the matching canonical graph audit (same revision, input hash and result hash). Exact row and catalog identities must agree; graph layer membership determines the explicit classification. Malformed flags, contradictory layer metadata and identity conflicts remain blocked. Recovery records raw and sealed classification plus the matching audit command in immutable approved-pricing compatibility evidence. The original frozen Contract is not rewritten. Saved eligible basis, applied amount, percentage, approved range and invoice reconciliation are still validated exactly; positive discount amounts and bases are never repaired by inference. Both Contract creation and authorized edits persist explicit classification to prevent new omissions.
