# Seal Optimizer-Derived Longitudinal Quantity from Agreeing Witnesses

A raw zero quantity on an optimizer-derived longitudinal Contract Item is a sentinel meaning that the optimizer derived the piece count, not evidence that zero material was contracted. Accounting may seal the positive canonical total linear meters only when the frozen optimizer plan and canonical Product Graph agree exactly with the scale-three sum of every persisted frozen Delivery/Product quantity for the same stable Product row; the wizard-era Delivery copy corroborates when present and blocks approval if it conflicts, but its absence does not invalidate otherwise complete persisted evidence. A zero invoice-item quantity is normalized to the same sealed meters only after this full reconciliation succeeds, while any positive invoice quantity must equal the sealed meters exactly. Missing or partial authoritative evidence, duplicate rows, incompatible units, over-allocation, malformed values, and any other conflict remain blocked for human review. Every operational view and document displays the reconciled canonical meters, while raw zero remains audit-only and unresolved rows show an explicit quantity-review state. The immutable approved-pricing evidence records the raw zeros, sealed meters, stable identity, individual Delivery witnesses and their aggregate, and normalization origin while leaving the Contract, invoice candidate, and Delivery snapshots unchanged, so normalization never rewrites commercial history or invents quantity.
