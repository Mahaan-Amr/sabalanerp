# Seal Optimizer-Derived Longitudinal Quantity from Agreeing Witnesses

A raw zero quantity on an optimizer-derived longitudinal Contract Item is a sentinel meaning that the optimizer derived the piece count, not evidence that zero material was contracted. Accounting may seal the positive canonical total linear meters only when the frozen optimizer plan, canonical Product Graph, and every persisted frozen Delivery/Product quantity for the same stable Product row are equivalent under the deterministic conversion registry selected by the recorded graph writer, writer version, graph schema, and calculation-policy version. New `canonical-wizard-save` and `legacy-migration` audit commands record `writerVersion: 1`. Historical commands created before that field existed are assigned only to the explicit legacy format version `0` when their command kind, immutable hashes, policy, and required backup provenance all match; any other missing or unknown provenance fails closed. The registry recognizes legacy-migration v0/v1 plus graph-v1/rounding-v1 as per-row half-up scale-two Delivery persistence, canonical-wizard-save v0/v1 plus graph-v1/rounding-v1 as the same historical scale-two rule, and canonical-wizard-save v0/v1 plus graph-v1/rounding-v2 as per-row half-up scale-three persistence. When the wizard-era Delivery copy is absent, a multi-row persisted conversion is ambiguous and blocks; a single persisted row remains sufficient authoritative evidence. When the wizard copy is present, its raw rows determine the historical per-row conversion and any conflict blocks approval.

This version supersedes the earlier requirement that persisted Delivery totals must always equal a direct scale-three sum. Equality is exact after replaying the recorded historical per-row rule; no tolerance or guessed rounding is allowed. A zero invoice-item quantity is normalized to the same sealed meters only after this full reconciliation succeeds, while any positive invoice quantity must equal the sealed meters exactly. Missing or partial authoritative evidence, duplicate rows, incompatible units, over-allocation, malformed values, and any other conflict remain blocked for human review. Every operational view and document displays the reconciled canonical meters, while raw zero remains audit-only and unresolved rows show an explicit quantity-review state. The immutable approved-pricing evidence records every raw witness, writer and policy versions, rule, exact differences, sealed meters, stable identity, individual Delivery witnesses and their aggregate, and initiating actor while leaving the Contract, invoice candidate, and Delivery snapshots unchanged, so normalization never rewrites commercial history or invents quantity.
