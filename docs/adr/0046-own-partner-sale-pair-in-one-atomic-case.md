# Own the Partner Sale Pair in One Atomic Case

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A Partner Seller makes one commercial sale that Sabalan must represent through two different truths: Sabalan sells to the Partner Seller at the approved wholesale price, while the Partner Seller independently sells to their Customer at a freely chosen retail price. Modelling these truths as either one ordinary Sales Contract or two independently editable contracts would permit the debtor, prices, Product rows, lifecycle, fulfillment, and customer output to drift or leave a partially created pair.

## Decision

`PartnerSaleCase` is the sole aggregate root and lifecycle authority. It atomically owns one canonical Product Graph, exactly one purpose-specific `SabalanToPartnerSaleRecord`, and exactly one customer-facing `PartnerCustomerSalesContract` whose explicit kind is `PARTNER_CUSTOMER`. The internal record is not a second `SalesContract`; it alone supplies Sabalan Accounting, receivable, collection, and fulfillment, while the customer contract reuses customer confirmation, PDF, print, and other ordinary contract capabilities without exposing wholesale evidence. Neither linked record may transition or replace its Case link independently.

Case identity, its operational number, the public customer-contract number, the private internal-record number, and every case-owned `productRowId` are immutable and never reused. Final Submit generates the identities and, in one idempotent transaction, reauthorizes the active Partner and owned Customer, revalidates exact inquiry/configuration and expiry evidence, and creates the Case, first revision, both linked Draft records, reciprocal links, snapshots, row bindings, and audit—or creates none of them. Database constraints must enforce one non-null unique record of each required purpose per Case and immutable reciprocal links, so no partial pair or orphan can commit or become visible.

The Case has one optimistic head revision and append-only revision and event histories. Every commercial change to parties, Product Graph, quantity, processing, tools, Delivery, inquiry binding, wholesale or retail price envelopes, discounts, tax, payment evidence, payment plans, or customer-facing content advances the coherent Case revision. A retail-price or customer-payment-plan-only successor regenerates the customer projection and advances the internal projection's expected Case revision while preserving its commercial content, Product Graph, wholesale evidence, and receivable; every shared-graph or Sabalan-side change regenerates both purpose-specific projections together. Product rows and Deliveries belong to the Case revision; both projections retain the same stable `productRowId` and may not own independent writable item or Delivery collections. Each revision carries a versioned integrity hash over its canonical commercial evidence, and sensitive reads, commitment, Accounting, and fulfillment fail closed and open evidence review when a projection's expected revision or hash disagrees.

The canonical lifecycle is monotonic: `DRAFT → AWAITING_CUSTOMER_CONFIRMATION → CUSTOMER_APPROVED → COMMITTED`, with atomic `CANCELLED` available before commitment and terminal `VOIDED` available only after the reviewed post-commit voiding workflow succeeds. Customer OTP records approval but does not commit the sale. The first customer-contract `SIGNED` or `PRINTED` fact records `committedAt` and its trigger exactly once, commits the Case and internal record together, and emits the single original realization event; the other fact cannot create a second debt or reporting event. `VOIDED` preserves that commitment and adds customer cancellation, internal voiding, dated financial and reporting adjustments, dependency evidence, and audit rather than rewriting prior facts. Compatibility `SalesContract.status` is only a monotonic projection of Case state.

Before Final Submit, Contract Creation Recovery remains private, leased recovery state and may be discarded under ADR-0043. Successful submission clears that recovery state. Once a Case and its commercial numbers exist, it can never be hard-deleted—even when Draft or Cancelled—so this aggregate deliberately applies a stricter retention rule than ADR-0040. Pre-commit cancellation transitions the whole pair atomically and retains identities, revisions, inquiry bindings, reason, and audit; a linked record can never be deleted or cancelled alone.

After commitment, the canonical Case revision is immutable and correction creates an explicit successor rather than overwriting either record. A retail-price or customer-payment-plan-only correction is requested by the Partner Seller, scope-approved by Sales management, saved once within one three-Tehran-working-day opportunity, and becomes effective only after fresh Customer confirmation; it does not enter Accounting or alter Sabalan's commercial projection. A Product Graph, Sabalan amount, Partner-to-Sabalan term, or voiding correction follows the processing and manager gates in ADR-0041, with Logistics first releasing or correcting physical dependencies under its own authority. The affected predecessor remains the effective evidence but is mutation-frozen after scope approval until every required system validation, Accounting verification, and Customer confirmation succeeds and the successor becomes effective atomically; a failed, rejected, or expired successor changes no effective truth.

Quantity-only and Delivery-only correction reuse the frozen wholesale unit-price evidence of the committed revision even after the original inquiry expires; only a changed price-bearing configuration requires a currently valid exact inquiry. A quantity reduction cannot fall below effective reserved plus physically dispatched quantity, active reservations are released by Logistics before correction, and dispatch decreases only after verified return and posted correction. Source geometry and remaining-stone children replay atomically in deterministic order, and any conflict rejects the whole successor. Approved financial, production, allocation, loading, Delivery, receipt, tax, and reporting evidence remains immutable and is adjusted through existing void, replacement, return, reconciliation, and verification gates. Cancellation and correction produce dated adjustment events under ADR-0011 instead of rewriting the original realization period.

An active Partner Seller may request post-commit voiding. When the Partner is suspended or terminated, an authorized Sales management or Admin remediation actor may originate the request under their own identity but cannot author Partner retail evidence. The requester cannot process or manager-approve the same Accounting chain, the Customer receives a safe non-vetoing notice, and the Case reaches `VOIDED` only when the customer Contract cancellation, internal record voiding, all required adjustments, and audit commit together.

The Partner Seller remains the immutable creator, Responsible Seller, and realized-sales-credit owner; a Price Responder receives no ownership or credit. This specializes ADR-0011 without changing its separation of technical creation, current responsibility, realized credit, and append-only reporting events. The shared Product Graph specializes ADR-0013 by making stable `productRowId` identity case-owned and identical across the internal, customer, Accounting, fulfillment, and output projections; similarity, ordering, or catalog identity can never substitute for that row identity.

### Precedence over general administrative override

The product owner explicitly approved the Partner-specific exception in [Resolve the ADMIN and correction-rule conflict](https://github.com/Mahaan-Amr/sabalanerp/issues/312). General system-wide authorization and Accounting-originated correction work must preserve these domain restrictions for Partner resources:

- An Admin may respond to a Price Inquiry only after an eligible, audited responder assignment, including a valid assignment to themselves; a general assignment override is insufficient.
- An Admin or Sales manager may perform named management and remediation actions under their own identity but may not author the Partner's Draft, retail price, customer payment plan, or other Partner-authored evidence on their behalf.
- The requester of a Partner Accounting correction or voiding chain may neither process nor manager-approve that same chain, including when the requester is an Admin. Audit does not waive this separation.
- Partner correction keeps the request and remediation paths defined above: retail-only correction remains Partner-requested and Sales-approved; shared, Sabalan-side, and voiding work keeps its Partner-specific authority and required Accounting and physical gates. Restoring Accounting-originated correction for ordinary Sales Contracts does not implicitly create that initiation right for Partner Cases.

Admin company-wide scope and all permitted named actions remain available with audit. These are explicit Partner domain exceptions applied through the central authorization decision, not a competing permission system. Ordinary internal Sales permissions and correction workflows remain unchanged.

### Partner dispatch-document audiences

The product owner resolved the conflict between the post-cutover paired-document rule in #256 and the price-free direct delivery in #323 during #334 acceptance on 2026-09-03: Partner dispatch retains atomic waybill-plus-statement issuance, but the customer waybill contains no economics or private internal-record number. The wholesale statement is restricted to authorized internal Accounting users and derives exclusively from approved Sabalan-to-Partner evidence. The final Customer remains only the physical recipient, never Sabalan's debtor. Retrieval, print, replacement, adjustment, and combined read models must enforce these audiences server-side; hiding a button is insufficient. Ordinary shipment documents, their pricing gates, and their access rules remain unchanged.

## Consequences

- All Partner Sale Case writers, transitions, corrections, projections, and downstream commands must enter through the Case aggregate with expected revision/state, command identity, compare-and-swap semantics, and transactional reauthorization.
- Accounting and fulfillment consume only revision-bound internal projections; OTP, public confirmation, PDF, and print consume allowlisted customer projections. A rendered customer-output snapshot is immutable evidence, not a writable Product Graph.
- Inquiry expiry or later Partner suspension cannot reprice or invalidate a successfully created Draft revision, although suspension blocks new mutations and commitment while it remains effective.
- Partner profile lifecycle is distinct from global User activation. Internal-to-Partner conversion enters restricted onboarding, requires explicit audited disposition of all open internal work and incompatible grants, transfers current responsibility for every historically correctable internal Contract to an active internal Seller without rewriting creator or realized credit, and becomes irreversible on that User after activation or the first Partner-owned Customer, Inquiry, or Case. Historical Contracts are never semantically converted or backfilled with inferred Partner evidence.
- Rollout uses a dedicated named cohort and Partner-profile eligibility rather than inherited feature permission. Enrollment pause stops only new cohort entry and activation; a separate emergency operational pause freezes every uncommitted Partner and responder mutation while preserving read, support cancellation, internal remediation, and committed Accounting and fulfillment.
- Migration and implementation must prove exact-pair constraints, immutable links and numbers, stable row identity, idempotent creation, one-time commitment, atomic pre-commit cancellation, reviewed post-commit voiding, successor correction, projection integrity, conversion and cohort gates, and the absence of every partial-success path.
