# Recover remaining-stone allocations only at authorized writes

Legacy remaining-stone children may carry an explicit zero material charge and physical consumption lineage without a canonical calculation snapshot. New contract saves and authorized edits reconstruct this evidence only when source ownership, physical allocation order, cutting prices, child-owned operations, final inventory and each row's monetary total can be reconciled; reads and historical migration tools remain unchanged unless explicitly opted in. Failed reconstruction aborts the complete save and preserves the draft, with row-addressed Persian guidance and a tracking ID instead of deleting rows, guessing prices or restoring consumed stock.

Replaying a witnessed allocation is deliberately not a new packing optimization: five 6cm strips historically cut from five 12cm remnants must not be silently repacked onto three remnants. The writer and recovery bridge use the same canonical packing engine. Intent snapshots retain the witnessed per-source distribution, any physical segments of a longer logical child piece, and the original inventory owner so later replay preserves consumption. The compatibility bridge may replay a two-dimensional layout only when that distribution is explicit, when a single consumed source accounts for every witnessed physical segment, or when the physical ledger proves exactly one segment per consumed source; canonical packing must then reproduce every generated remainder identity, geometry and cutting amount. Without that evidence, automatic inference remains limited to same-length longitudinal layouts whose capacity, generated-remainder identities and total piece count determine one unique distribution (including partial-stock consumption). Unsupported or ambiguous layouts fail closed and require explicit reconstruction evidence, not a heuristic. Legacy geometry comparisons tolerate only machine-precision floating-point residue; money uses the repository's existing whole-toman reconciliation independently for every row.

Manual guidance scopes recovery through stable row references and consumed/generated physical identities, distinguishes the original material owner from a secondary-remnant producer, and gives source-first rebuild order. Missing or cyclic lineage has no guessed deletion order. This preserves the existing formal accounting-correction permission boundary for finalized contracts and does not authorize a background history rewrite.
