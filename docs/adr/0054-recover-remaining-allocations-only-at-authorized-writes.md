# Recover remaining-stone allocations only at authorized writes

Legacy remaining-stone children may carry an explicit zero material charge and physical consumption lineage without a canonical calculation snapshot. New contract saves and authorized edits reconstruct this evidence only when source ownership, physical allocation order, cutting prices, child-owned operations, final inventory and each row's monetary total can be reconciled; reads and historical migration tools remain unchanged unless explicitly opted in. Failed reconstruction aborts the complete save and preserves the draft, with row-addressed Persian guidance and a tracking ID instead of deleting rows, guessing prices or restoring consumed stock.

Replaying a witnessed allocation is deliberately not a new packing optimization: five 6cm strips historically cut from five 12cm remnants must not be silently repacked onto three remnants. Canonical intent snapshots can retain the witnessed per-source distribution and original inventory owner so later replay preserves consumption. The compatibility bridge accepts same-length longitudinal layouts only when source capacity, generated-remainder identities and total piece count determine a unique distribution (including partial-stock consumption); unsupported or ambiguous layouts fail closed and require explicit reconstruction evidence, not a heuristic. Legacy geometry comparisons tolerate only machine-precision floating-point residue; money uses the repository's existing whole-toman reconciliation independently for every row.

Manual guidance scopes recovery through stable row references and consumed/generated physical identities, distinguishes the original material owner from a secondary-remnant producer, and gives source-first rebuild order. Missing or cyclic lineage has no guessed deletion order. This preserves the existing formal accounting-correction permission boundary for finalized contracts and does not authorize a background history rewrite.
