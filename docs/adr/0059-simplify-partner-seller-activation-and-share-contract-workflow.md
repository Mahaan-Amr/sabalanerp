# Simplify Partner activation and share the ordinary contract workflow

ADR-0057's staged activation package remains readable for historical records, but it is superseded for new Partner conversions. An authorized manager converts an eligible active User from that User's profile by selecting one currently eligible price responder and confirming removal of incompatible internal access. The transaction creates the active Partner profile, debtor account, profile identity snapshot, responder assignment, fixed Partner capability bundle and audit evidence together. It does not require or expose commercial identity verification, Partner-specific commercial terms, credit terms, cohort membership or release-readiness input. Existing internal responsibilities remain historical and transferable; they do not authorize new internal work. The current system administrator cannot convert themself, an ADMIN must first lose that role, and a terminated or already converted identity cannot be converted again.

Partner price approval is authority for a catalog pricing subject, not for one exact contract configuration. Cut-stone families use an approved Toman-per-square-meter rate for the main catalog stone; prepared and volumetric products use their catalog sales unit. Optional dimensions are retained as immutable inquiry history but quantity, dimensions, cutting, tools, finishing, payment, customer and project do not invalidate the approval. A voluntary successor inquiry may carry an optional note; only an approved successor supersedes the still-valid predecessor. Approved prices remain fixed for 48 hours.

Partner contract creation uses the ordinary seven-step workflow vocabulary and shared Design System components: contract date, customer, project, products, delivery, payment and digital confirmation. Customer and project are contract facts, so independent inquiries do not require them and multiple inquiry batches may proceed concurrently. Project selection is mandatory at final submission. The Sabalan wholesale amount is calculated by the ordinary canonical product graph, replacing only the main-material rate with the approved inquiry rate and retaining ordinary cutting, tool, finishing and other component calculations. Paid remainder material is never charged twice. Customer retail price, margin, the atomic Partner Case pair and scoped projections remain Partner-specific additions.

The customer payment plan and the ordinary confirmation SMS/link/code belong to contract creation. A Partner-to-Sabalan payment plan is assigned later by Accounting; its absence does not block Case creation or customer confirmation, but continues to block final financial approval. Existing committed records and evidence are never repriced or rewritten.

A mistaken direct conversion can be reverted only while the profile is active, has no customer, inquiry or Partner Case, and has not crossed an irreversible lifecycle boundary. Reversion is revision-bound and audited: the profile returns to `PENDING`, the exact role and access rows captured by the latest direct conversion are restored, and all profile, account, assignment and conversion evidence remains intact. Once commercial evidence exists, correction must use the normal suspension or termination lifecycle instead of changing the historical identity.
