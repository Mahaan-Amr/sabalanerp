# Use one primary accounting book per legal entity with analytical dimensions

Each legally registered reporting entity owns its own stable Primary Accounting Book across Fiscal Years. Sabalan's current legal entity activates one such book, while Branches, Departments, Cost Centers, Projects, Contracts, Warehouses, Parties, and similar internal boundaries remain governed analytical dimensions on entries rather than separate ledgers or duplicated charts of accounts. The model remains ready to add another independently scoped book when a genuinely distinct legal entity enters the platform, without activating consolidation or a complete intercompany engine now. This preserves one accounting source of truth and avoids fragmented customer, bank, and company balances, at the cost of making typed dimension rules, dimension-aware authorization, and dimension-aware reporting explicit parts of the accounting design.
