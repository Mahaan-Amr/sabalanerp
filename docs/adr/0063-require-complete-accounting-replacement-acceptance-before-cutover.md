# Require complete accounting replacement acceptance before production cutover

SabalanERP may build and verify the accounting replacement through internal milestones, but it will not partially replace Sepidar in production. The accepted release boundary includes the general ledger and chart, journals and financial statements, customer sales and receivables, treasury and bank reconciliation, purchases and payables, inventory and cost accounting tied to stone production, fixed assets, approved-payroll posting and settlement, VAT and Taxpayer System workflows, statutory books, year-end close, migration and parallel reconciliation, authorization and tamper-evident audit, backup and recovery, automated QA, and a comprehensive manual acceptance checklist completed by the User. Consolidation, elimination, a complete intercompany engine, and budgeting remain outside this first replacement release. Production authority transfers only after the whole included boundary is implemented, reconciled, and accepted; no completed internal milestone is by itself permission for a partial server rollout.
