# Use governed manual and automatic journal posting

One explicitly authorized actor may prepare and post a sensitive manual, opening, adjustment, closing, or correction voucher without a second person, subject to strong confirmation, mandatory reason where applicable, immutable audit, and exception notification. Operational sales, receipt, purchase, and similar workflows may post automatically only after their accounting gate succeeds through an active versioned rule, idempotent source identity, complete evidence, valid mappings, and an open period; any ambiguity fails closed into an exception queue. Posted vouchers are immutable and enter statutory reports, while correction occurs only through linked reversal or corrective vouchers. This avoids both uncontrolled automation and mandatory two-person ceremony while retaining individual accountability.
