# Assign immutable posted voucher numbers per book and fiscal year

Every voucher receives an immutable internal identity and reference at creation, but receives its statutory sequential number only when posted. That number is unique within the Primary Accounting Book and Accounting Fiscal Year, is never changed or reused, and is not released by voiding or reversal; any gap remains explainable through system evidence. Daily numbering and presentation order are projections rather than accounting identity.
