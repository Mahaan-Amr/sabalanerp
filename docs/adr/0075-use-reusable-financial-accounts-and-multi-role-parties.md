# Use reusable financial accounts and multi-role parties

Each real bank account, cash fund, or governed financial instrument is one reusable typed Financial Account under eligible control accounts rather than a duplicated Subsidiary Account or free-text value. Likewise, one Accounting Party may hold Customer and Supplier roles while receivable and payable remain separate and offset only through an authorized posted journal. Historical identifiers and roles are effective-dated and are never rewritten merely because an account closes or a counterparty gains another role.
