# Settle open items through immutable allocations

Receipts, payments, credits, and advances remain independent posted events that may be allocated partially or fully across one or more traceable open items. Unallocated value remains a Party advance or credit rather than a negative receivable, and the system never moves it automatically across invoices or Contracts. Remaining balances and aging derive from posted immutable allocations, while correction uses linked reversing allocations instead of editing historical settlement evidence.
