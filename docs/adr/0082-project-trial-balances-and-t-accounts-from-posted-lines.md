# Project trial balances and T-accounts from posted lines

Trial balances and T-accounts are rebuildable projections of posted journal lines rather than writable balance entities. Trial balance supports two-, four-, six-, and eight-column presentations at Group, General, Subsidiary, and permitted Detail levels, with opening, turnover, ending balances, governed dimension filters, and drill-down to journal and source evidence. T-account presents debit, credit, chronological running balance, filters, and the same traceability. Draft-inclusive views remain explicitly labeled previews under ADR-0074.
