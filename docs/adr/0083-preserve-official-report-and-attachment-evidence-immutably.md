# Preserve official report and attachment evidence immutably

An official report export freezes its dataset, parameters, cutoff, mapping and policy versions, actor, generation time, source identities, and integrity hash. Persian RTL PDF and Excel outputs are renderings of that one dataset and remain reproducible and verifiable after live data changes. Attachments associated with posted Accounting truth are immutable, hash-addressed, malware-scanned evidence with type, uploader, timestamps, retention, supersession links, and audited access; a corrected document creates a successor version instead of replacing the original. Viewing and exporting remain separately authorized capabilities within the simple Accounting access profiles.
