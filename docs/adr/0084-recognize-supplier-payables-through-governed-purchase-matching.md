# Recognize supplier payables through governed purchase matching

A Supplier role is activated on the existing Accounting Party from the first approved purchase document or other governed supplier relationship without creating debt. Inventory and fixed-asset invoices require ordered, received, and invoiced evidence; services and expenses require accepted-service and invoiced evidence. A purchase without an order is a reasoned authorized exception, while quantity, price, tax, or Supplier differences fail closed. Supplier invoices preserve gross amount, discounts, attributable costs, recoverable and non-recoverable taxes, deductions, retention when applicable, rounding, and net payable; returns and corrections use new reference-linked documents rather than mutation.
