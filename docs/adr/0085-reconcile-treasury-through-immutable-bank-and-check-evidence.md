# Reconcile treasury through immutable bank and check evidence

Bank movements enter through supported API adapters, versioned CSV or Excel mappings, or controlled manual entry as immutable, idempotent Bank Statement Lines. Reconciliation associates them with independently recorded Treasury Transactions and posted entries; automation proposes matches but never confirms ambiguity, and internal transfers are not external cash flow. Receivable and payable checks preserve Sayad identity, custody, endorsement and assignment chain, deposit, clearing, bounce, return, replacement, and cancellation evidence. Operational check states affect the ledger only through versioned posting rules.
