# Separate cash funds, petty cash, and open-item settlement

Each real cash box is a governed Financial Account with custodian, counts, and separately posted shortage or overage. A Petty Cash Advance belongs to a named custodian with limit, settlement period, Cost Center, and supporting-document obligations and remains an asset until explicitly settled. Supplier payments, advances, receipts, and credits are independent Treasury Transactions allocated immutably across open items; surplus is never silently moved to another invoice, and correction uses reversing allocations.
