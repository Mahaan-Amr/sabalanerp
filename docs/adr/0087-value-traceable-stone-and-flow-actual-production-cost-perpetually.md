# Value traceable stone and flow actual production cost perpetually

Blocks, slabs, pieces, and traceable remainders use specific identification with preserved origin and transformation lineage; homogeneous consumables use moving weighted average. Sale reservation, physical movement, and accounting valuation remain separate linked facts, incompatible units are not aggregated, and valuation methods change only prospectively. Actual costs flow perpetually from raw material through work in progress and finished goods to cost of goods sold; versioned cost pools allocate direct and overhead costs by recorded operational bases, normal waste is absorbed, abnormal waste is expensed, and incomplete evidence fails closed. Cutover begins from a physical-count, item-level, Sepidar-reconciled opening dataset whose uncertainties remain exceptions rather than saleable stock.
