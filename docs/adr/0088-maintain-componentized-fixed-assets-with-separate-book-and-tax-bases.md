# Maintain componentized fixed assets with separate book and tax bases

Fixed assets and significant components receive stable register identities with class policy, physical identifiers, location, custodian, documents, counts, and append-only lifecycle evidence. Effective-dated class policies govern recognition thresholds, useful life, residual value, and straight-line, declining, or units-of-production methods. Book depreciation is posted to the statutory ledger; a separately reproducible tax basis supports tax reporting and deferred-tax analysis without becoming another general ledger. Costs accumulate in construction in progress until evidenced ready for use, ordinary repair is expensed, qualifying improvements and component replacements are capitalized, and transfer, impairment, class-wide revaluation, sale, loss, and disposal use explicit events rather than mutation.
