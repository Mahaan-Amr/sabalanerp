# Derive statutory books through versioned regulatory formats

Legal journal, general ledger, and subsidiary ledgers are non-editable projections of posted journal lines with statutory sequence and full drill-down, never independent books. Each printed or electronic package records its effective official schema, validation rules, cutoff, source citation, integrity hash, and authority receipt. A successor regulatory format affects only its declared periods, while historical outputs remain reproducible under the exact version originally applied.
