# Reconcile versioned tax obligations and compliance duties

VAT declarations reconcile sales tax, eligible purchase credit, referenced corrections and returns, ledger controls, Taxpayer System state, payments, carryforwards, penalties, exemptions, and non-creditable amounts before submission. A shared Tax Obligation and Compliance Calendar foundation also tracks payroll tax, legally applicable withholding, performance tax, prepayments, refunds, adjustments, deadlines, attempts, receipts, and settlements, while each tax retains its specialist calculation and filing rules. External submissions are asynchronous and idempotent, and neither rejection nor transport failure rewrites posted economic truth.
