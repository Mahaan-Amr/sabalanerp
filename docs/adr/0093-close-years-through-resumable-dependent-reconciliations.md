# Close years through resumable dependent reconciliations

Period and year close use a resumable dependency graph from document completion through every applicable subledger, treasury, inventory cost, fixed asset, payroll, remeasurement, schedule, tax, suspense, trial-balance, and report-snapshot check. An upstream change explicitly invalidates affected downstream evidence. Year end closes temporary income and expense accounts into the approved retained-result account and creates the next opening from accepted closing balances plus detailed open subledger items. A single authorized Accounting Manager may close or reopen with strong confirmation, reason, and immutable audit; correction never rewrites the accepted closing or opening evidence.
