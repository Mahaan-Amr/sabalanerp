# Preserve prior-period, estimate, and legal-archive evidence

Versioned quantitative and qualitative materiality policy governs whether a closed-period error is adjusted in an open period or enters a controlled prior-period restatement case. Original and restated reports, comparatives, opening effects, tax effects, disclosures, decisions, and correcting entries remain linked and visible. Estimate and contingency cases distinguish recognized provisions, disclosure-only exposures, and remote items while preserving assumptions and reviews. Posted vouchers, books, statements, declarations, receipts, attachments, and audit evidence remain searchable and hash-verifiable under effective retention policies and legal holds; expiry alone never triggers automatic destruction.
