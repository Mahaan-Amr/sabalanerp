# Gate Accounting release on comprehensive automated and manual evidence

Activation of the Accounting replacement requires behavioral, property, integration, contract, concurrency, idempotency, migration, authorization, security, recovery, end-to-end, visual RTL, export, architecture, and regression automation plus a comprehensive Persian manual acceptance package. Production-like load proves unique numbering and effect, safe retries, correct locking, interactive latency, and asynchronous heavy exports. Operational readiness includes a Persian exception center for posting, mapping, tax, bank, check, close, suspense, migration, audit, backup, queue, and connector failures. Production activation of the Accounting replacement under ADR-0063 requires that two parallel periods reconcile, migration, cutover, safe pause, and restore rehearsals pass, every mandatory scenario has fresh evidence, and no critical or high-severity defect remains open. Ordinary code deployments follow [ADR-0144](0144-make-product-qa-and-periodic-drills-independent-of-release.md).
