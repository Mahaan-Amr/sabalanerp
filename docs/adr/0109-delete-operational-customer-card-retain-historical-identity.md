# Delete the operational Customer card while retaining historical identity

The product owner explicitly wants permanent removal of a Customer card even when it has Contracts or Partner Sale Cases, and chooses to retain those records with their historical customer details. This supersedes ADR-0108’s history-as-deletion-blocker policy: a Customer’s operational CRM membership can be permanently removed, while its referenced historical identity and card data are frozen and retained. Existing Contract and Case IDs, links, revisions, integrity hashes, financial evidence and commercial numbers are preserved without reassignment or cascade.

Unused Customers may still be physically removed with unused card children. A referenced Customer loses its physical operational membership row and remains only as a historical identity, with an immutable snapshot tied to the deletion receipt. This is not erasure of all personal information: the approved historical details remain. There is no restoration path. Existing records continue to resolve that identity; new CRM selection, new Cases and unrelated new Contracts cannot use it. A Contract generated by an already-existing Partner Case may retain that Case’s identity so ongoing work remains possible.

Deletion retains current Admin / explicitly authorized Company Manager scope, preview fingerprint, mandatory reason, simple final confirmation and immutable audit. Historic card fields and children cannot be edited or reactivated. The UI must enumerate retained history rather than telling the user those records will also be deleted. Source and local verification do not authorize production publication.
