# Separate ordinary contract approval from financial settlement

The agreed ordinary Sales Contract model separates Sales approval, Customer acceptance, and financial settlement. Creation and a successful commercial edit produce یادداشت and invalidate earlier approval authority for the current version while retaining history. The existing Sales checkmark is the sole Sales approval: without Customer acceptance it produces پیش‌نویس; once both approvals exist for the same version, the Contract becomes قطعی automatically, without a second Sales approval. امضا شده represents Customer acceptance without Sales approval. Both approval orderings are permitted. Customer acceptance may be digital or an Accountant's audited recording of the Customer's actual paper signature; a paper-signature record does not assert customer-phone verification.

Accounting may view ordinary active Contracts and print their original, Accounting, workshop-score, and custom versions throughout the commercial lifecycle. Before قطعی, ordinary financial actions, Accounting flags, and new correction requests are unavailable; paper-signature recording remains available. Viewing, printing, and recording paper acceptance do not create a financial edit lock. Before any financial record exists, Sales may edit repeatedly in every commercial stage, with each successful save resetting the lifecycle and approval authority. Any existing financial record preserves the controlled Accounting-originated correction boundary and immutable financial evidence. Approved correction opens a multi-save Sales editing period: every successful save resets commercial approvals, and renewed قطعی closes the period and returns the complete correction difference to Accounting verification. This deliberately replaces the existing one-save opportunity for the new flow; permissioned ongoing correction decisions and verification must remain available despite the commercial reset. The existing three-Tehran-working-day expiry is retained: expiry closes the editing period while preserving saved changes, and further editing requires renewed Accounting authorization.

Partner Seller workflows are outside this change. Existing cancellation, expiry, deactivation, deletion, and recovery safeguards remain applicable. The new lifecycle applies to Contracts created after rollout and to an existing Contract only upon a successful edit; untouched legacy Contracts retain their current behavior and history. Logistics visibility and dispatch under the new flow require commercial finality and full financial settlement through actual Accounting receipts applied to the obligation; an uncleared check or the Sales payment plan does not qualify. Current Logistics checks financial approval rather than settlement, so this is an intentional new gate. A current summary that counts uncleared received checks is insufficient settlement evidence. If settlement is lost after loading preparation, further loading and exit are blocked until settlement is restored; plans and evidence remain preserved, and an already recorded physical exit is never rewritten. Workshop execution is not implemented and is not introduced by this decision.

This proposal changes the SIGNED/PRINTED commercial meanings used by ADR-0011 and the signed-contract edit restriction, while preserving historical evidence. For the new flow, commercial finality alone does not realize Sales performance: the first successfully persisted Accounting financial record is the realization trigger, including a saved draft, without waiting for financial approval or settlement. Opening an unsaved form, a failed save, printing, a flag, or paper-signature recording does not qualify. Retries or later records must not realize the same sale again. If deletion or voiding leaves no valid financial record, a dated reversal neutralizes the realization without erasing history; later record creation must not double-count Sales value. Historical reporting events remain preserved.

Sales will additionally own a configurable expiration duration, initially ten calendar days, changeable only by Admin and Manager authority in the Sales workspace. A Contract without its first financial record by its deadline becomes منقضی شده. The clock begins at system creation for a new Contract, or the first successful new-flow edit for a legacy Contract; the selected commercial Contract date is not the clock origin. Edits never renew the deadline. Each Contract retains its assigned duration, so later setting changes affect only newly created or newly adopted Contracts. An authorized Sales Manager or Admin may renew an expired Contract with a mandatory reason and a fresh deadline; renewal resets it to یادداشت and invalidates earlier Sales and Customer approvals. Ordinary Sellers cannot bypass expiry by editing. Once the first financial record has been successfully saved, later deletion or voiding never makes this Contract subject to that expiration deadline again; reporting reversal and financial workflow controls remain independent. This expiration policy is distinct from the retained three-working-day financial correction period. The user confirmed the complete shared understanding on 2026-10-01.
