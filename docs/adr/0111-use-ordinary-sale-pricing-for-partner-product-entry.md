# Use ordinary Sale pricing for Partner product entry

Partner product entry follows ordinary Sale configuration, validation and canonical material-consumption pricing. Catalog prices are optional: a positive seller-entered price remains valid when an imported Product has no base price. Customer prices and Sabalan responder prices remain independent; each agreement computes its own mandatory percentage charge from its own material price, while trusted fixed ancillary rates and the paid-remainder zero-material rule remain shared. Reusing a catalog amount or an invented QA price would either block legitimate production products or misprice the agreement.

This supersedes ADR-0062's finished-part Stair and consumed-mother-unit Slab price bases, and its rule that the mandatory amount is identical in both commercial projections. New technical revisions identify the ordinary pricing basis and require successor pricing evidence when an old basis changes. Historical finalized graphs and their amounts retain their recorded semantics. Prepared Products keep their ordinary selected sales unit; cut stone uses canonical consumed material area, which may differ from finished area.

The production catalog and finalized agreements are not rewritten as part of this source repair. Product duplication creates fresh graph identities and retains existing remainder children with their original source. Independent services have separate recovery and price evidence: the customer rate is editable, the Sabalan purchase rate comes from the shared trusted catalog, and no stone inquiry is manufactured. Contracts may contain services without stone. Service execution quantities are scheduled separately and fully distributed like ordinary Sale; service rows contribute to financial totals and contractual outputs, while physical fulfillment remains derived only from the stone graph.
