# Reactivate cancelled ordinary Sales Contracts with fresh approvals

The owner confirmed on 2026-10-11 that reactivation keeps the same ordinary Sales Contract number, Customer and Items, returns the Contract to یادداشت, and requires fresh Sales approval and Customer acceptance while preserving prior cancellation and approval history. Before the first financial registration an edit-authorized Seller may reactivate directly; after the first financial registration fresh Accounting authorization through the correction workflow is required, including the ability to review a cancelled Contract. A short reason, actor and execution time are mandatory.

Reactivation retains the assigned commercial deadline. The owner rejected renewing a cancelled Contract whose applicable deadline has passed and requires a new Contract instead. This prohibition applies specifically to cancelled Contracts; the ordinary renewal of a merely expired Contract under ADR-0110 remains available. Renewal must not serve as an indirect bypass for an expired cancelled Contract. The deadline remains applicable only within its existing domain scope, distinct from the Accounting correction period.

The ordinary Contract detail page exposes the permissioned reactivation command. After reason entry and successful authorization/reactivation, the User enters the full Contract editor with the retained information. The existing final-step wizard command follows the same rules. Partner Sales and administrative deactivation are outside this change.

Accounting reviews and retains still-valid existing financial evidence and actual receipts for the same Contract; reactivation does not revive voided documents, duplicate receipt application, erase financial history or reset physical shipment evidence. An amount change follows the existing financial correction controls. Fresh Accounting reactivation authorization must follow the latest cancellation rather than reuse an opportunity approved before it. The existing single correction chain, three-Tehran-working-day authorized editing period, renewed-finality closure and Accounting verification remain applicable.

The reporting effect of a reactivated Contract remains suspended in یادداشت and during renewed commercial approval. It returns only when the current version is commercially final again and at least one valid financial record exists, using the current commercial amount without double counting or rewriting original realization/cancellation history. Validity for this reporting rule retains the current financial-registration meaning, including a successfully saved non-voided draft; it is distinct from financial approval, settlement and dispatch authority. Editing the amount or changing financial evidence before renewed finality must not restore a suspended sales contribution. This decision concerns reporting after cancellation/reactivation rather than changing the realization trigger for all ordinary Contracts.

Historical ordinary cancelled Contracts follow the fresh-approval reactivation model. If a Contract has neither an assigned commercial deadline nor any history of financial registration, reactivation assigns the current standard deadline once, measured from reactivation. An existing deadline is retained and, if applicable and passed, prevents reactivation. Existing or historical financial registration keeps the Accounting authorization boundary even when its documents have since been voided or removed; missing legacy deadline data must not fabricate a retroactive expiry or erase financial evidence.

For a cancelled Contract whose applicable deadline has passed, the detail page offers «ایجاد قرارداد جدید از این اطلاعات». Customer and Items enter the normal creation form for review, with a new Contract number allocated through the ordinary creation rules; prior approvals and financial evidence are not transferred, and the original Contract remains preserved. Prefill does not itself create or finalize a Contract and remains subject to ordinary current Customer, Product, calculation, permission and recovery rules.

The owner confirmed the complete shared understanding and authorized local implementation on 2026-10-11. This decision supplements ADR-0110 and ADR-0117 for commercial cancellation/reactivation and leaves the administrative inactive-Contract lifecycle in ADR-0040 intact; it does not confirm implementation, validation or authorization to deploy.
