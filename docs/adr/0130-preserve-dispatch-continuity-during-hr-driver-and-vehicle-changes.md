# Preserve dispatch continuity during HR driver and vehicle changes

Accepted during the HR driver and company-vehicle requirements interview on 2026-10-14; implemented locally, with no production release. Ordinary eligibility and assignment changes take effect at the exact recording instant, permitting multiple changes in one day without rewriting earlier periods; future assignments remain schedulable, while backdated corrections are outside ordinary commands. This avoids treating distinct same-day actions as one effective timestamp and preserves the meaning of historical dispatch evidence established by ADR-0036.

Reassignment and assignment termination must wait until open Guard or dispatch operations involving any affected driver or vehicle are resolved. Ending driving activity or archiving a vehicle closes its current assignment in the same confirmed action and follows that same open-operation restriction. Eligibility suspension instead takes effect immediately, leaves the vehicle assignment intact, and blocks subsequent steps requiring eligibility without automatically deleting or cancelling the dispatch case: urgent withdrawal of driving authority must remain possible even when operational work is unfinished.

Ending driving activity combines ending HR eligibility, archiving the driving profile, terminating the current assignment, and explicitly cancelling future assignment plans in one confirmed action, requiring each corresponding permission. Reinstating archived profiles or vehicles returns them to draft for explicit readiness-checked activation and never reopens old assignments. Future plans may be changed or cancelled before they start with history retained; conflicting future plans require an explicit disposition instead of silent cancellation.

All forms in HR vehicle operations and Personnel driver eligibility omit manual reason entry, as explicitly requested in the interview. Automatic action evidence records the actor, time, and actual changes; it must not be represented as an explanation supplied by the user. This clarifies the reason-evidence requirement of ADR-0036 for these HR-owned surfaces while retaining separate permissions and historical evidence; it does not change external Guard registry forms.

When a future assignment's start or end is due but open operations or driver/vehicle readiness prevent execution, the plan becomes actionable as needing attention without automatically closing the preceding assignment. Execution after the blocker clears requires explicit user confirmation and takes effect at the actual execution instant. Ordinary vehicle details and licence facts may be corrected during open operations without rewriting their frozen snapshots; plate changes must wait, and licence corrections immediately revalidate readiness and prevent subsequent eligibility-dependent steps when invalid.

Non-overlapping successor assignment plans account for the anticipated terminations in earlier approved plans; execution still checks the actual pairing and stops for review if that anticipation was not fulfilled. At a shared boundary the scheduler processes endings before starts, uses the actual execution clock, and participates in the existing recovery and deployment maintenance write-admission and drain boundary. Scheduling the end of a current pairing preserves its assignment period until execution and does not create a duplicate assignment. A draft vehicle with retained history can be archived again without first making it operational.
