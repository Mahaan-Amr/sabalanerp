# Use effective-dated assigned rest windows for personnel attendance

The first discovery round confirmed replacing the report's flat daily rest-minute deduction with one shared start/end rest window and a date-effective entitlement per stable Personnel identity. For entitled personnel, the part of the rest window within required work hours is excluded from both expected time and ordinary worked time whether inside or outside the premises; absence crossing its edges contributes only the portions outside the window, with no second rest deduction. Personnel explicitly without rest receive no such exemption.

Expected hours continue to come from the canonical HR work schedule under ADR-0017, never a competing report-owned schedule. Shortage is limited to required work hours and must not double-count overlapping lateness, early departure or intermediate absence. A missing schedule cannot produce a definitive shortage. Historical dates use the rest window and entitlement effective on that date; editing today's policy does not silently rewrite the past, and approved payroll remains immutable under ADR-0022.

Confirmed examples for an 08:00–17:00 workday with assigned 13:00–14:00 rest: continuous presence yields eight ordinary worked hours and no shortage; absence only from 13:00–14:00 also yields eight ordinary worked hours and no shortage; absence from 12:45–14:15 yields seven and a half ordinary worked hours and thirty minutes of shortage. Physical presence remains distinct from ordinary worked time.

The second discovery round confirmed that approved mission time covers required time and counts as mission work, distinct from physical presence. Union overlapping physical/mission evidence and exclude assigned rest so no minute is counted twice. A guard note alone is not an approved mission. Approved leave excuses the authorized required-time portion without becoming worked or physical-presence time; paid and unpaid leave remain distinct and this report does not decide payroll deductions. For leave 12:30–14:30 with assigned rest 13:00–14:00, the user explicitly approved sixty minutes of leave consumption. This requires deliberate reconciliation with any leave-balance owner rather than silently changing only a report total. Pending leave decisions do not become approved leave or final unjustified shortage.

Night workers do not need a separate rest window in this version. Only actual overlap with the common rest window is excluded: a 22:00–06:00 shift has no overlap with 13:00–14:00 rest. A shared window crossing midnight is permitted; calculations remain attached to the shift start date and do not invent a new overnight rest interval.

Known attendance portions remain visible when entry/exit times are unknown; incomplete duration means incomplete work/shortage, not a guessed time, zero, or a whole-day shortage. Valid manual attendance is not missing presence merely because no scan exists.

The third discovery round confirmed removing mandatory separate Guard confirmation for ordinary valid device movements: known identity, valid time and consistent movement order may directly support calculations. This deliberately replaces the earlier provisional-until-Guard-confirmation design for ordinary movements, including its Q8 provisional ordinary-event proposal; it does not remove review of ambiguous direction, identity, source-time or attendance conflicts. Manual registration and reasoned audited correction remain available. System acceptance is not a fabricated Guard signature. ADR-0132 remains applicable to source evidence and ambiguity; implementation of this accepted decision must explicitly reconcile ordinary-event confirmation terminology and existing historical confirmation evidence without silently rewriting them.

Shortage and presence outside the work schedule remain separate; thirty minutes of each must not automatically cancel each other. Compensation or approved overtime is an independent HR decision. A scheduled day with no attendance evidence becomes an unresolved missing-presence case after the shift, rather than final whole-day unjustified shortage; an authorized decision confirming actual absence and no applicable authorization can finalize the required-time shortage. Rest management follows the existing explicit personnel-work-schedule permission and audit boundary, not generic Guard attendance access. Report generation consumes effective policy rather than creating arbitrary per-request expected hours or rest rules.

The fourth discovery round confirmed a distinct unassigned-rest state for existing and new personnel: HR may bulk-set explicit entitlement or no entitlement, but must not infer it from the old report's flat deduction. Recording physical attendance remains available; definitive net work and shortage require the assignment to be resolved. Rules start at an explicit effective date. Earlier dates use the legacy report method and must be labeled accordingly; an unknown historical report option cannot be presented as an exact reconstruction. Existing PDFs remain unchanged and a date range spanning policy changes uses the method effective for each date.

Reports for an unfinished workday are interim: future required hours are not shortage, completed intervals remain available, and an open attendance interval receives no fabricated exit. Final day totals await shift completion and resolution of incomplete evidence. Calculations retain precise source time, union overlapping coverage before accounting, and floor to whole minutes only at each daily metric total; period totals sum those displayed daily minute amounts rather than independently rounding the whole period.

Ordinary valid device movements leave the single/bulk Guard confirmation and mandatory confirmation-handover workflow. Ambiguous events, unknown-time cases and unresolved missing presence retain resolution or reasoned handover to a named responsible person. Existing historical human confirmations and source revisions remain evidence rather than being deleted or replaced by fabricated acceptance.

When approved leave conflicts with valid physical attendance (for example leave 10:00–12:00 and actual return at 11:00), preserve both source records and surface the overlapping portion for authorized leave correction. Known presence remains visible, but final leave consumption and shortage of the conflicting portion remain unresolved; one minute cannot be finalized as both work and consumed leave. Reporting itself cannot cancel the approved leave.

All Q1–Q18 recommendations and the consolidated design were approved by the user on 2026-10-21 for implementation and tests in the existing local Docker project only. Existing PDF visual design, fonts, pagination and generation settings, and report permissions remain preservation constraints; descriptions and calculated values must accurately reflect the new policy. Approved payroll and the three previously disputed attendance cases are not altered by this design. Cloud mutation, new attendance activation, payroll deduction, push and deployment remain unauthorized. The current runtime has no leave-balance ledger; this change exposes effective consumption per approved source in the report and does not fabricate balance debits or alter the approved request duration. Any subsequent implementation authorization is scoped to local Docker unless the user explicitly expands it.

Factual absence decisions apply only to uncovered required time: already approved partial leave or mission remains credited, and the reviewer confirms there is no additional authorization for the uncovered part. The decision never converts an authorized hour into shortage. A later change to attendance, schedule, assignment or authorization invalidates the previous source fingerprint without deleting its audit evidence. Completed days with unresolved times or authorization conflicts remain reviewable even when they have some attendance evidence.

On 2026-10-21 the user removed the manually entered reason from rest settings. Policy/assignment writes retain the actor, effective date, values and audit event; when no reason is supplied the server stores a factual action description, not a fabricated explanation from the user. Historical supplied reasons are retained. This does not remove the reason/factual-evidence requirement for attendance corrections or absence decisions.
