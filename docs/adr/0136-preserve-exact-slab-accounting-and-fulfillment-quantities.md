# Preserve exact slab accounting and fulfillment quantities

Slab area is a measured contractual fact: `2.95 × 1.75 × 3 = 15.4875 m²` must reach financial approval, loading reservation and shipment quantity evidence unchanged. New approvals seal matching exact frozen slab and graph witnesses under `exact-slab-quantity-v1`; their agreed monetary amounts and precise source snapshots are never recalculated. This supersedes scale-three rounding for slab area in ADR-0045, while the recorded historical optimizer conversions in ADR-0042 and existing immutable approvals remain authoritative.

Quantity transport and evidence columns widen to `Decimal(65,30)`, retaining the previous 35 integer digits available to aggregate quantities and allowing 30 fractional digits. Values beyond this explicit capacity fail closed before persistence rather than being silently rounded. The bound accommodates current finite canonical geometry results without imposing an arbitrary four-digit fix. Reservation and monetary-allocation quantity arithmetic uses scaled integers; decimal quantity calculations use a separate high-precision constructor, leaving the monetary rounding policy untouched. Monetary allocation keeps its existing scale-twelve arithmetic and exact final-remainder rule.

Existing quantities with at most three fractional digits retain their scale-three canonical text and integrity hashes. Additional fractional digits are preserved in serialization and integrity checks. Migration only widens storage: it does not backfill previously rounded evidence, resolve review cases, rewrite contracts, activate fulfillment workflows or alter release/recovery gates. Previously approved contracts require their existing correction workflow for any commercial change.

Local acceptance covers real authenticated financial approval and canonical Logistics allocation/finalization, exact partial reservations, rejection of an excess as small as `0.0001`, preserved source snapshots and amounts, and persisted financial/logistics pricing binding in a rolled-back transaction without enabling the global paused statement workflow.
