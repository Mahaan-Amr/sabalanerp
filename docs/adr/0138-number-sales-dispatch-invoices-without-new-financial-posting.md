# Number sales dispatch invoices without new financial posting

The numbered sales dispatch invoice represents one finalized Logistics loading and must be issued before Guard records physical exit for any included driver. It uses an independent sequence beginning at 1, preserves its number on repeated downloads, and requires voiding the predecessor and issuing a new number for a correction rather than rewriting the previously issued invoice.

The source contracts must have valid financially approved records and share one customer and accounting counterparty, though their projects may differ. Ordinary and Partner sales are covered; Partner amounts derive from Sabalan's sale to the Partner, preserving ADR-0105's counterparty boundary. Issuance creates no new financial posting: the invoice documents the loaded goods against existing financial evidence rather than recognizing a second sale or receivable. It is available in Accounting and Logistics; the production/purchase contract PDF and workshop PDF remain Accounting outputs.

One invoice covers the whole loading across all drivers and includes their details. Ordinary and Partner invoices share one continuous sequence starting at 1 without annual reset. Accounting owns issuance and voiding; authorized Logistics users can view and download. Existing print variants remain available, and the workshop variant retains its delivery schedule while excluding financial prices and payment plans.

The invoice uses the Accounting print appearance and contains invoice/loading identity, customer and counterparty, source contract/project references, loaded product quantities and dimensions, approved rates and amounts, related operations and services, allocated discount, and the net total for this loading. It excludes contractual delivery and payment schedules. Amounts use the existing approved shipment calculation without entering new charges or recognizing a new financial posting.

Accounting issues the invoice only after all current portions of the loading have completed Accounting acceptance. Item and quantity selection belongs exclusively to Logistics loading; the invoice follows those saved quantities exactly and cannot add, remove, or independently edit cargo. Existing loading composition restrictions remain in place.

After physical exit, a change must first complete the applicable shipment correction workflow; only then may the old invoice be voided and a new numbered invoice issued, without rewriting Guard exit history. Voided invoices remain available in history with an explicit void status. Historical documents also remain available with their invalid status when their financial source loses validity; loss of source validity must not silently rewrite the issued invoice.

An active, valid invoice for the whole loading is an additional prerequisite for Guard exit of every included driver. If any portion still awaits Accounting acceptance, no driver in that loading may exit: all portions must be accepted and the whole-loading invoice issued first. This deliberately trades independent vehicle departure for a complete invoice before departure; it supplements every existing per-vehicle, driver, waybill, and exit-authorization check and does not replace them. A voided invoice cannot satisfy this prerequisite. The user confirmed the consolidated design and approved implementation on 2026-10-28 after reviewing the three PDF samples.
