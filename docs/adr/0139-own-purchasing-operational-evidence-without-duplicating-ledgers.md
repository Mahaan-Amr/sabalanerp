# Own purchasing operational evidence without duplicating ledgers

The owner approved the Persian purchasing prototype and authorized its real implementation in issue #425. The accepted prototype is preserved separately on `codex/purchasing-prototype-accepted` at `7768a87a`; production routes are implemented independently using the platform Sabalan Design System and the HR workspace presentation. This decision records domain ownership, not completion or production readiness of every accepted requirement.

Purchasing owns Requests, supplier Orders, physical Receipts, quality acceptance and rejection, actual Returns, Exchanges, change requests and purchase-advance requests. These are versioned operational documents with explicit typed links and exact decimal quantity text. One Order can fulfill several Requests; each share preserves both the purchased quantity/unit and the originating demand quantity/unit. Different quantities or units require an explicit conversion confirmation. Cross-request capacity checks include shares in the current command. Contract Product identity and the canonical Partner Case projection remain the authoritative sources of demand; Partner self-service cannot create Purchasing Requests.

A firm customer-related Order requires a current, registered, nonvoid financial record matching the source commercial revision; this is not a requirement for paid or financially approved status. Partner demand uses Sabalan's internal financial record and the existing canonical financial-preparation witness, never the Partner's private retail amounts. Historical purchases retain their actual date and an explicit financial exception where evidence was absent; recording reality never creates permission for a new Order.

Received, quality-accepted, pending, rejected, returned and replacement quantities are independent facts. Only net accepted quantities fulfill demand. Rejection does not imply physical return. A replacement requires a distinct Exchange and Receipt, with independent quality acceptance, and is not another supplier Order. Direct delivery records a CRM-selected recipient of the actual Customer; different Customer shares are capped separately and replacement destination remains bound to its original Receipt. A raw-stone need can be supplied without declaring its finished Product ready.

Accounting owns Supplier Invoices, payable Open Items, actual payments, settlement and valued inventory effects. Purchasing never inserts these records merely because an Order, Receipt, quality acceptance or advance request was entered. Existing-source links are globally unique and preserve a hash and snapshot of the original source; they do not recreate it. Explicit Accounting grants continue to protect financial-source reads and links, including for a technical Administrator. Purchasing Manager/Admin authority does not bypass Accounting's existing authorization contract.

Each mutation uses a fresh command key, a stored immutable outcome, an expected document version where applicable, and a bounded transactional Purchasing graph lock. This initial conservative lock protects allocation and duplicate-effect invariants across Requests. It serializes Purchasing commands and may be replaced with a documented finer-grained protocol after contention evidence warrants it. All runtime operations reuse the application Prisma client. Financial/source locks follow the canonical Partner-then-Contract order.

Operational events retain actor identity snapshots, prior/new document ownership, status and data, plus a chained hash. Events, accepted command outcomes and linked-source evidence cannot be updated or deleted through the database. Changes after commitment create new evidence and explicit outcomes rather than deleting historical receipts or financial facts. Notifications are in-app only and are reauthorized against current resource access before display. Purchase prices and commercial history are redacted on the server for sellers and warehouse users.

The accepted scope remains tracked in #425. The initial operational slice has since been extended locally under ADR-0140: quality acceptance publishes canonical physical custody and contract protection, explicit Accounting handoff recognizes invoices against that same custody, and secure private attachments stay within the database backup boundary. This does not authorize production promotion. Valued/consumed inventory returns and already-valued direct-destination transfers still require their owning Accounting/Inventory correction workflows; Purchasing must fail closed rather than fabricate a reversal or second receipt. Current local acceptance evidence and remaining boundaries are recorded in `reports/qa/purchasing-completion-local-20261101.md`.
