# Simplify Purchasing entry without erasing Request evidence

The owner approved two starts: a real warehouse/customer need, or an independent catalog price inquiry. Both collect and compare supplier proposals. Only a real need may continue toward purchase. All authorized internal requesters may start catalog inquiries; price visibility and entry keep the existing buyer/accountant boundaries. Catalog data reuses Sales product identities and presentation without requiring Sales authorization or treating Sales prices as purchase prices.

The normal UI removes the separate Request form. “Continue to order” saves the buyer selection and creates the existing auditable Request in one transaction and idempotent command. The subsequent Order review remains explicit and enforces current financial readiness, quantities and authority. Existing Request documents, identifiers, links, events, recovery and historical routes remain intact. This is not automatic firm ordering.

Catalog inquiries distinguish general prices from dimensions/processing-specific proposals. Reusing a general proposal for a precise need requires recorded supplier confirmation of those specifications and explicit destination/quantity review. Expired proposals remain evidence and require actual reconfirmation. A market inquiry cannot directly become a Request; a later real need may explicitly reuse a compatible valid proposal.

Same-code/name rows may be grouped visually while retaining row identities, specifications and amounts. This grants no cross-contract/stock aggregation. The dashboard prioritizes actionable work; rare operations and historical lists remain available through details and the sidebar. Canonical Sabalan components, Persian labels, existing price privacy, physical custody, accounting ownership and audit invariants remain unchanged.
