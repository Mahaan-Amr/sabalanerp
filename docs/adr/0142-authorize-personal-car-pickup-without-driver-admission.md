# Authorize personal-car pickup without driver admission

The owner selected the following policy during the requirements interview on 2026-11-18. The owner confirmed the consolidated design and authorized implementation, clarifying that only Contracts actually selected for this loading may be extracted; other Contracts of the Customer or Project are excluded. Implementation is local; no production release is authorized by this decision.

«راننده شخصی» is a distinct personal passenger-car pickup by the Customer or their representative, selected in the Logistics driver step. Each loading is exclusively personal pickup or other-driver transport, never a mixture. Customer and source Contract identities derive from the actual Logistics selection; operators enter no driver name, mobile, plate, or driver-registration details. Ordinary and Partner sales are supported, and Accounting and Guard see the selected Contracts and Customer; Partner sales explicitly show both the final Customer and Partner without merging their identities or changing the accounting counterparty.

This is a narrow exception to the Guard-admission/shared-pool prerequisite in ADR-0006, the admission identity model in ADR-0036, and the driver verification policies in ADR-0131. Personal pickup requires neither Guard entry verification nor driver fingerprint or OTP verification. Accounting explicitly grants exit authority, after which Guard sees the pickup under خودرویی → خروج مجاز and records the physical exit. Keeping this exception separate avoids inventing a reusable driver identity or weakening internal, external, or متفرقه transport controls.

Contract financial eligibility and required dispatch documents remain applicable, including ADR-0138's valid whole-loading invoice prerequisite. Financial-record issuance and sales dispatch invoice issuance remain explicit operations separate from exit authorization. Clicking «صدور مجوز خروج» only checks the applicable prerequisites and grants exit authority; it must not create or approve a financial record, issue an invoice, establish settlement, or require personal-pickup fingerprint/OTP verification. Buyer exit-notification SMS remains enabled after the physical exit; it is independent of verification OTP. The existing expiry, revocation, single-use authorization and physical-exit evidence controls continue to apply.

Logistics starts both ordinary and Partner loadings from «بارگیری جدید». Partner delivery search uses the name of the final Customer who owns the Contract and Project. Only deliveries with a positive available quantity can start a new loading. An existing Partner loading retains its selected delivery and Contract when opened from the loading list.

Before finalization, Logistics may switch a draft between personal pickup and other-driver transport. Switching clears the previous driver selection and driver-specific quantity allocations and requires quantity revalidation. Finalized records follow the existing explicit correction workflow rather than in-place edits. Each personal-pickup loading represents exactly one exit occurrence, with one exit authorization consumed by one Guard exit confirmation; two independently departing passenger cars require two personal-pickup loadings.
