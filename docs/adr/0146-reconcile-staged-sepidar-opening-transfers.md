# Keep staged Sepidar openings and cheque histories reconciled

Accounting approves a dedicated Opening Transfer Clearing Account for staged customer openings. The transfer retains an explicit reconciliation of that account to the remaining source dispositions and requires Accounting approval before final posting.

Migrate the opening carrying position of each reviewed cheque together with its evidenced subsequent events so that a historical custody position cannot appear as a current open cheque. Preserve the actual event sequence and link it to the original instrument and affected ledger entries. An opening must not create a second receipt or advance.

Apply ADR-0064 to the Target Chart of Accounts and ADR-0128 to customer opening reviews. Assign a project or contract only when source evidence supports the relationship and the target identity is eligible. Preserve the opening amount independently when no attribution can be verified. Keep production admission subject to the protected release procedure and its reviewed migration and recovery evidence.
