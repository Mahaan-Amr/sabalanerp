It addresses the held local operation `a0d2baba08ec7e004d1458675799db6a` after LOCAL allocation. `ABNORMAL_RETIRED` is a proposed negative terminal label for that operation, not successful qualification, original closure, release readiness, or deployment approval.

[ADR-0039](../adr/0039-require-verified-zero-data-loss-deployments.md) requires fail-closed verified recovery; [ADR-0131](../adr/0131-compatible-online-deployment-and-independent-recovery.md) retains those lease and recovery invariants and protects unknown and active resources. ## Preconditions for any separately approved implementation

1. Establish an explicit, independently reviewed succession contract and implementation for this post-LOCAL state. It must define what replaces the original owner's non-transferable obligation, how authority is issued and revoked, and how the previous owner is fenced without overlap. User permission and OS handle possession alone are not such an authority.
2. Bind the exact operation, owner and supervisor birth identities, original Job and lease, source, scope and resources. Inventory every allocated scope, authority/claim, reader, child and relevant database session or prepared transaction. Unknowns remain quarantined and negative; preserve the current baseline and business data rather than rewinding them.
3. Demonstrate continuous exclusion and fencing for all relevant resources, including throughout any required backend return and until actual successor acquisition. A successful sequential test of an OS lock after release does not prove continuous custody. A potential unfenced window between release and reacquisition is **unproven** and is a blocker even when the same file identity is observed before and after. Do not assume a path, process ID, or a historical snapshot proves current ownership. Host crash, reboot, an apparently free lock or a lost handle is neither a fence nor proof of closure; remain `HELD` until a fresh operation-specific census, quarantine and effective fencing are established.
4. Define the precise canonical restoration actions, interruption handling, durable intent and outcome journal, crash reconciliation, independently observed exit and pipe closure where available, conservation audit, and failure handling. No raw pipe release, guard weakening, unreviewed signal, replacement owner, deletion, or clearance based solely on elapsed time is permitted.

## Proposed boundaries, not commands

* **Before action:** Revalidate all identities and a live, continuous exclusion mechanism. Establish no overlapping actor and retain independent, operation-specific custody and quarantine. If any necessary identity, reader, lock, database observation, journal or fencing proof is missing, remain `HELD`.
* **Controlled retirement and restoration:** Only a future qualified mechanism implementing the reviewed contract may fence the prior owner and perform the exact restoration. It must bind each action to current custody and stop on any failure or uncertain effect. No historical EOF or successful qualification may be synthesized. Failure, interrupted action, lost custody or uncertain outcome retains quarantine, data, original evidence and recovery obligations.
* **After restoration:** Independently verify the exact relevant service identities and configuration, database and file conservation, SQLite against the appropriate baseline, and absence of related active readers, sessions and children. A future `ABNORMAL_RETIRED` record must retain the unknown historical fields and name any unresolved quarantine. Such quarantine continues to block release, reuse, access and promotion of its affected scope unless a separately reviewed path resolves it. Only the separately reviewed succession contract can define how a valid release of the original obligation and lease is authorized and proven. This draft provides no release mechanism.

## Bounded current-actor threat model

This model concerns the operation's known entrypoints and accidental overlap; it does not claim resistance to an administrator deliberately ignoring every contract. The operation index identifies 17 scopes: nine HTTP/serving, five jobs/control and three capture/control. A historical diagnostic observed nine distinct locks across those scopes and 52 absent paths; that snapshot is neither current custody nor a complete actor inventory.

The original Root and several local QA entrypoints acquire the Windows serial lease through `scripts/lib/serial-physical/host-lease.mjs` in the execution worktree. Its `deploy/scripts/online-runtime-owner.py` and `backend-runtime-owner.py` enforce scoped native locks and claims; kernel and task admission guard connected writer paths. These guards protect their specific entrypoints, not every actor able to affect a mounted volume or database. In this checkout, `package.json` exposes direct Compose `docker:local:up` and `docker:local:down`; the browser workflow invokes the former without acquiring the Windows serial lease. The running services and direct, authorized Docker or SQL paths likewise cannot be presumed to consult a new file marker. These are capabilities, not evidence that an unobserved write occurred. The candidate and retained backends mount the same acceptance volume, so a holder inside either one cannot be assumed to survive both recreations. Some local services use restart policies; reboot is not evidence of closure or of fencing.

For a controlled window, one executor, a stopped runner and a coordinated prohibition on external Compose and SQL commands may be explicit operational preconditions; merely having such a command available does not mean an actor is executing it. These conditions do not address already-running automatic workers or service restart policies. Determine each active runtime's actual image, role, process and permitted writes, then prove its native admission/drain and scoped locking under the operation's current identities. A separate durable holder must survive any required backend return until successor acquisition, or the boundary remains unproven. A source change alone does not modify the already-loaded owner. Missing, torn or stale state, unknown active actor, holder loss, interrupted acquisition or reboot remains `HELD`; acquisition of a lock alone is not permission to reuse quarantined scopes. This is a proposal for review, not an implementation or an executable procedure; no general-purpose gate or workflow change is authorized by this draft.
