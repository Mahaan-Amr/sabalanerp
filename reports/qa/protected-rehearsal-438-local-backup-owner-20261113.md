The app daemon's stop and the six Desktop containers' final health could **not** be confirmed. No global WSL shutdown, broad process kill, Desktop setting change, QA network creation, or W: write was attempted. Host probing stopped at this instability.

**NO-GO:** A no-data simultaneous-daemon probe is unstable, and the WSL endpoint cannot prove off-computer backup independence. Before any #432 restore or six-service run, independently recover and observe the exact app/backup/Desktop process state, diagnose memory and kernel networking pressure, prove stable simultaneous owner/controller operation, then use a physically independent QA backup owner for the controller's remote verification and retention gates. Do not treat this local fixture-like endpoint as production admission.
